## Recording rules

- Capture an actual browser at normal zoom, with real page turns and system audio. Keep notifications, credentials, account details and `.env` off screen.
- Show the source on every data change: **Synthetic example** or **PayPal Sandbox — no real money**. Never cut between these sources as though they were one imported record.
- Leave short quiet gaps for the status tones and device narration. Do not replace device speech with a polished voice the app cannot produce.
- Actual API waiting may be shortened with an on-screen **“Waiting time shortened”** label. Never insert a prerecorded response into the app or disguise cache as a fresh model call.
- Use English narration/subtitles for every shown operation and result. Keep the original quote visible; translate it in a separate subtitle, without replacing its source text.
- Export at less than 3:00. Check the entire exported video, including the final frame and sound, before uploading. Runtime estimates are not a measured final duration.
